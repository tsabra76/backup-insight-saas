# BackupInsight - Product Launch Plan

## Core Features
- AI-powered backup analysis that finds patterns in your files
- Personal asset valuation from your backup content
- Insights drawn from metadata and file content
- Automated reports on backup health

## How it Works
1. You connect your backups to BackupInsight
2. Our system analyzes the contents of your backed up data
3. AI generates insights about your digital patterns, interests, and value
4. You receive actionable reports monthly

## Target Market
- Digital collectors with extensive backup systems
- Music enthusiasts with large libraries
- People who understand their data's worth but don't know how to quantify it
- Privacy-conscious individuals with valuable personal content

## Launch Strategy
- Start with a free beta for 100 users
- Offer premium features at $9.99/month
- Focus on marketing to collectors, creators, and tech-savvy people
- Partner with backup software companies

This leverages your existing investment in backups while extracting value from that data.
